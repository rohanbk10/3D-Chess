How to Play

  • Standard chess rules, including castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and draws.
  • You command Polished Marble (white). Click a piece, then a glowing square. Captures are shattered.
  • vs AI pits you against the Cathedral Automaton (Easy, Normal or Hard). Pass & Play is for two players on one device.
  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, or pick a camera preset below.
  • Spell Hint highlights a strong move. Undo rewinds your last move.
  • Your game is saved in this browser automatically.

Shortcuts: H hint, U undo, M sound, 1-4 camera, Esc deselect.
